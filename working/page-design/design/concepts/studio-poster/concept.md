# Studio Poster

**Big idea:** Full-bleed dark editorial banner with heroic numerals framing pitch capacity and active gate deadlines.

**Signature moves:** The Band (full-width mint callout), Heroic Numerals, White and Mint Pill Badges.

**Borrowed from the reference:** Mint accent #3BE891, dark ink typography, pill geometry, high-contrast flat surfaces.

**Not borrowed:** name, product words, copy, images, logo, font files.

**Axes:** composition = poster bands; emphasis = numbers as heroes; colour = one confident colour field; type = oversized heavy display; density = airy and calm; chrome = top nav

## Critique

### Round 1
- First seen: Heroic banner stating 14 active tenders.
- Template test: High-contrast mint poster band breaks stock Universal Theme feel immediately.
- Removed: Dense table view on fold; promoted headline capacity instead.
- Tells: Evaluated generic AI tell warnings; kept restrained flat geometry.
- Changed: Adjusted contrast on eyebrow text to ensure strict WCAG compliance.

### Round 2
- First seen: The eye lands on the bold 32px display text and primary CTA.
- Template test: Looks like an editorial agency workflow tool rather than a generic database grid.
- Removed: Removed extraneous region borders around stat blocks.
- Tells: No generic AI tell remaining; spacing sits on a clean 4px grid.
- Mobile: Hero band stacks cleanly at 390px with zero horizontal scroll.
